Layers

STILL FRAME — nothing animates. We're molding the master image. Everything glowing is additive light, not artwork. The mark is the same logo alpha stacked six times at increasing blur with plus-lighter — a real bloom pyramid. The centre accumulates past pure white and blows out; the three bars stay transparent so less light gathers there, which is why they read as darker notches inside the blowout rather than as painted bars. Nothing is opaque and nothing has an edge. The Spill layer is the light the mark casts back onto the sphere. Drag any orb to reposition — coordinates print to the console.
